A note from Dr Priya

Thank you for reading with me.

We began with a simple question: how can we measure something that nobody can see? Together, we followed Maya from a single answer sheet to a full estimate of her ability, and along the way you met the ideas that sit behind modern tests and questionnaires.

Chapter 1Why hidden traits are hard to measure, and why IRT exists.
Chapter 2One logistic idea that grows into a whole family of models.
Chapter 3How the likelihood finds the hidden numbers.

If one idea stays with you, let it be this: an IRT model is a careful way of turning answers into evidence, and every estimate carries both a value and a measure of how sure we can be.

Chapters 4 and 5, on the algorithms in depth and on checking whether estimates can be trusted, are on their way.

One last favour. Please tell me what worked and what did not. Your feedback takes about ten minutes and shapes the next edition of this book.
